No hot water: what to check while you wait

Losing hot water in an Ohio winter is urgent, and a leaking tank is more urgent still. Our dispatch is open 24/7 and same-day visits are common, and while you wait you can check the breaker on an electric unit or the pilot window on a gas unit.

If you smell gas, do not touch switches or the heater. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside, then call us once everyone is safe.

Signs the heater needs service

These point to a repair, a flush, or the end of the tank's life.

  • ✓Water is lukewarm or runs out after a few minutes
  • ✓A popping or rumbling sound from the tank
  • ✓Rusty or cloudy hot water
  • ✓A pilot that goes out, or a burner that cycles on and off
  • ✓Moisture or a puddle under or around the tank
  • ✓A tripped breaker every time the heater runs

What hard water does to Barberton heaters

Barberton's water treatment plant draws from the city reservoir, and the city's latest water quality report lists hardness at 170 mg/L, with a range of 100 to 250. That is in the hard range, and it matters here because minerals settle as a crust at the bottom of a tank.

A crusted tank heats slowly and uses more fuel, and on a gas unit the crust can cause the popping noise you hear as steam bubbles escape. Flushing the tank helps, and a water softener cuts the buildup at its source.

Repair or replace: the honest test

Storage tank heaters commonly last around 10 to 15 years. If the heater is past 10 and the repair costs a large share of a new unit, replacement usually wins. A leak from the tank body is never a repair.

A failed element, thermostat or thermocouple on a younger tank is worth fixing. We tell you which side of the line you are on, and if replacement is better, our water heater replacement page explains the process.

Temperature and pressure safety guidelines

The Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends setting a water heater thermostat to 120 degrees Fahrenheit to reduce scald risk. Every tank also needs a working temperature and pressure relief valve, and a leaking relief valve is a sign to call.

Do not plug or cap a relief valve or its discharge pipe. That valve is the last safety device on the tank.
