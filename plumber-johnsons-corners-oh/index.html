Where Johnson's Corners sits

A neighborhood guide places Johnson's Corners at the corner of Wooster Road and 31st Street, about two miles west of downtown Barberton, with State Route 21 along its western edge. It sits in ZIP 44203 and is served mainly by Barberton City Schools.

Waltz, Conrad and Breitenstine parks are listed as the neighborhood's parks. The guide says the name comes from an 1817 settlement, which is local history and not a plumbing fact. For hot water safety, Consumer Product Safety Commission guidelines recommend a thermostat setting of 120 degrees Fahrenheit.

Houses from the mid century

The guide lists a median build year of 1955, with Colonial Revival and ranch-style homes most common, plus some bungalows and Cape Cods. It says smaller homes were designed for people who wanted to downsize.

Ranch homes may have a full basement, a partial one or a slab, and we do not assume which. Where you have a basement, a sump pump and a clear floor drain are worth testing each spring.

What to do each spring in a mid-century home

These checks take a few minutes and can prevent a bigger repair.

  • ✓Pour a pitcher of water into the floor drain to see if it runs freely
  • ✓Look at the water heater for rust, pooling or a hissing sound
  • ✓Test the sump pump by lifting the float
  • ✓Find the main water shutoff and make sure it turns

Hot water and fixtures

Smaller homes often have one water heater in a utility corner. If it fails, our repair and replacement pages help you choose.

A bathroom fixture that leaks at the base or a faucet that drips can be repaired in most cases. We explain the price before work begins.

Near the city edge

Johnson's Corners is on the western side of the city, so check whether your address is in Barberton or in neighboring Norton. City limits do not follow ZIP codes, because both use 44203.

If you are in Norton, see our Norton page. Water and sewer arrangements can differ by address, and your bill shows who supplies you.
