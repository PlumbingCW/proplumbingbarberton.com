Old canal ground and low basements

The Ohio and Erie Canal ran through southeastern Norton from 1827 to 1913. Low ground near old waterways tends to hold moisture, so properties in that part of town are the ones to watch for damp basements.

If your basement stays damp after heavy rain, a working sump pump and a clear discharge line are the first defense. Ask about a battery backup before storm season, because power often fails in the same storm that fills a pit.
