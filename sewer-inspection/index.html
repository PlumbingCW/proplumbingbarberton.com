Sewer Inspection in Barberton, OH

A sewer camera inspection shows what is inside the lateral before you buy, repair or cut a trench. You see the footage, not a guess.

Call (855) 575-2890

What a sewer camera inspection is

A waterproof camera on a flexible rod travels down the cleanout and records the pipe from the house toward the street. The video shows cracks, offsets, root growth, sags and buildup. A locator on the camera head tells us how far along the line each problem sits.

It is a planned service, so we schedule it at your convenience. If you are dealing with an active backup instead, the faster route is sewer line cleaning with a camera pass afterward.

When an inspection pays for itself

These are the situations where we recommend a camera before anything else.

  • ✓You are buying a house with a lateral older than the furnace
  • ✓The same drain has clogged more than once in a year
  • ✓You see a yard depression or a damp strip over the pipe
  • ✓A neighbor had their lateral replaced
  • ✓You are planning a remodel that adds a bathroom

Buying an older Barberton home

A home inspector usually checks the visible plumbing but not the buried lateral. In a city founded in 1891, with about 11,800 housing units in the 2020 census, the lateral is the one major system a buyer often skips.

A camera run before closing can reveal a root-filled clay joint or a sag that holds water. That gives you a price to negotiate or a reason to walk, and it costs a small share of a lateral replacement.

What a camera cannot tell you

A camera sees the inside of the pipe, not the soil around it. It cannot measure the strength of a pipe wall, and a line that looks fine today can still shift after a wet spring.

It also only travels as far as the cable reaches and as far as the bends allow. If the camera stops at a sharp turn or a collapsed section, we say where it stopped and what we could not see, instead of calling the line clean.

After the inspection

If the pipe is sound, you leave with proof and a clear conscience. If there is a defect, we explain the options on our sewer line repair page and can give you a written quote.

We inspect lines for homes in Norton and Copley as well. Ask the dispatcher about same-day scheduling.

How we grade what the camera sees

Sewer camera findings are coded with the NASSCO pipeline assessment method, which labels cracks, offsets, roots and sags in a standard way. A coded report is easier to compare with a second opinion or share with a seller or a lender.

Ask for the recording itself. A plumber who will not hand over the video is a plumber to question.
