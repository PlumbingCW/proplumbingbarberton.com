Who we are

About Barberton Pro Plumbing

We are a call-only plumbing service in Barberton, Ohio. You phone, a dispatcher listens and a licensed plumber is sent.

Call (855) 575-2890

Barberton Pro Plumbing serves Barberton, Ohio. We handle emergency and planned plumbing for homes and small businesses in Barberton, Norton and Copley.

There is no booking form on this site. Every request starts with a phone call, because plumbing problems change by the minute and a dispatcher can ask the questions a form cannot.

How we work

Our dispatch line answers 24/7. We send licensed and insured plumbers, and you hear the price before work starts. After-hours calls cost more than weekday visits, and we say so on the phone.

We write each page on this site for the local area. We name the Barberton Reservoir, the hard water in the city's latest report and the rules of the building department because those details change what we recommend.

How to check any plumber

Ask for the plumber's license details and verify them with the State of Ohio's licensing board before you sign. Summit County's contractor tips say the same, and a reputable plumber will not mind the question.

Get the price in writing, ask what the warranty covers and keep the receipt. Those habits protect you whether you hire us or someone else.

What we do not do

We do not do engineered fire sprinkler systems, medical gas, structural foundation repair or mold remediation. We tell you when a job belongs to another trade.

We also do not post reviews, ratings or awards on this site, because we want every claim here to be one you can check. Call and ask us anything about the work, the price or the plumber who will come.

From phone call to repaired pipe

  1. 1

    You call

    The dispatcher takes your address, the problem and any safety risk, such as gas, sewage or standing water near electricity.

  2. 2

    You get a price

    Before anyone starts work you hear the price, plus anything that could change it, such as hidden damage.

  3. 3

    A plumber comes

    A licensed and insured plumber is sent. For emergencies we book the next available plumber, and same-day appointments are common when one is free.

  4. 4

    The work is checked

    The plumber tests the repair, cleans the work area and explains what to watch for.

What we promise and what we do not

We promise a clear price before work starts and plain answers about what is wrong. We do not promise a fixed arrival time, because traffic, weather and a busy night all move the clock, and a promise we cannot keep helps nobody.

We also do not quote a firm number for work nobody has seen. We give you a price range when we can and a firm number once the plumber has looked.

Local rules we work within

Barberton's building department has handled plumbing permits and registered plumbing contractors, and the city lists the 2017 Ohio Plumbing Code and the 2019 Residential Code of Ohio. In 2026 the city announced that Summit County's Division of Building Standards takes over state-required code enforcement.

Permit rules vary by job, so we ask the right office before work starts on anything that may need one, such as a water heater swap or sewer repair.

Why the phone and not a form

A burst pipe, a backed-up sewer and a dead boiler in January are all problems that get worse with every minute. A dispatcher can ask whether the water is off, whether anyone smells gas and whether the basement floor drain is rising, and then send the right plumber with the right parts.

A web form cannot ask a follow-up question, and it sits unread overnight. That is why every page on this site points to one number, and why the line stays staffed around the clock.

Who we serve

Most calls come from homeowners in Barberton, where many houses sit in the valley beside the old canal and have older drain lines and older water heaters. We also take calls from small businesses such as shops, offices and restaurants that need a commercial plumber on short notice.

Beyond the city we cover Norton and Copley, and the service area page lists the ZIP codes and estimated distances for each.

Hard water and what we recommend

Barberton's latest water quality report lists hardness near 170 mg/L, about 10 grains per gallon. That is hard water, and it leaves scale on heating elements, inside tankless units and around faucet aerators.

For that reason we often suggest a yearly tank flush, and for some homes a water softener. We explain the cost and the upkeep on the phone before anything is installed.
