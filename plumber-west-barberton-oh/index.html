Around Lake Anna

A neighborhood guide describes West Barberton as centered on Lake Anna Park, with Tuscarawas Avenue as its main street and bus stops along Wooster Road and West Lake Avenue. The city lists the Lake Anna Gazebo at 615 W. Park Ave.

Lake Anna is a spring-fed lake in a 21-acre park, and the park hosts the late September Mum Fest. A large sports complex borders the west side of the neighborhood.

Bigger houses mean more to look after

The guide reports a median build year of 1904 for West Barberton and says homes overlooking the lake tend to be larger. Styles it lists include Colonial Revival and American Foursquare.

A larger house usually has more bathrooms, more fixtures and a longer run of pipe, so there is more that can wear out. That is general plumbing information, and the real picture only appears when a plumber looks.

When to ask about repiping

Low water pressure, rusty water or repeated leaks in several spots can mean the supply lines are near the end of their life. Before anyone recommends replacing them, a plumber should confirm which material is in the house and where the trouble starts. Any replacement should follow the Ohio Plumbing Code, which builds on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Our repiping page explains how the decision is made. If one leak is the only problem, leak detection often finds it without opening more walls than necessary.

Hot water in a bigger house

A larger home often needs a bigger water heater or a second unit. Sizing depends on how many people live there and how many fixtures run at once.

Read about water heater replacement and tankless options before you decide. Hard water at about 170 mg/L also means any heater benefits from regular flushing.
