Sewer backup: what to do first

If sewage is coming up in a basement floor drain or a ground-level tub, call right away. Our dispatch is open 24/7, and while you wait you should stop using water everywhere, including toilets, showers and the washer, because each flush adds to the flood.

Keep out of the wet area and keep children and pets away. Leave electrical panels alone if the floor around them is wet, and tell the dispatcher about it.

Signs the main line is blocked

A main sewer blockage affects the whole house at once, and the lowest drain shows it first.

  • ✓Floor drain or basement toilet backs up
  • ✓Several fixtures drain slowly at the same time
  • ✓Gurgling in a toilet when the washer discharges
  • ✓Sewage smell in the basement or yard
  • ✓Water pools around the cleanout cap

How Barberton's sewer system raises the stakes

The city's sewer network includes combined, sanitary and storm sewers, according to its own stormwater information. After heavy rain a loaded system can push water back toward low basements, and a partly blocked lateral makes that backup more likely.

Barberton sits in a valley beside the Tuscarawas River, with creeks feeding in from several sides. The June 2019 flooding showed how quickly water finds basements here, so a clear lateral before storm season is cheap insurance. Ask the city which part of the line is yours before you pay for work on the street side.

Why roots keep coming back

Roots seek moisture and enter through a joint the size of a thread. Cutting them clears the line for months or a year, but it does not close the entry point.

If you have cleaned the same line twice in a year, a sewer inspection with a camera is the sensible next step, and a sewer line repair may be cheaper than a third cleaning.
