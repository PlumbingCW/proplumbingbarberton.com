Kitchen Sink Drains Slowly but Is Not Clogged

A slow kitchen sink with no visible clog is usually a partial blockage in the line below the trap. Grease, a disposal problem or a dishwasher connection are the usual suspects.

By the Barberton Pro Plumbing team. Updated 2026.

Slow is a warning, not a mystery

Water that leaves the sink slowly is telling you the pipe is narrowed. Grease builds on the pipe wall from cooking fat, and coffee grounds and starch settle on top, leaving a channel the size of a straw.

That channel drains a sink slowly, then clogs when something solid arrives. Acting now costs less than waiting for a full backup.

Dishwasher and disposal connections

A dishwasher drain hose connects at the disposal or the sink drain. If the knockout plug was left inside the disposal, or the hose is kinked, water backs up in the sink.

A disposal full of old scraps traps water too. Run cold water, grind small amounts and flush with plenty of water. If the problem persists, our garbage disposal service team can check the unit and the connections.

What a plumber does differently

A short cable opens the line, and for grease a jetter scrubs the wall. A hand plunger or a store-bought snake often moves a clog without removing the film that caused it, which is why the sink slows again in a month.

If the same sink slows repeatedly, schedule hydro jetting or a camera check. It removes the coating and shows whether the pipe is sagging.

Barberton local note

Who is responsible for which pipe in Barberton

If the slow drain affects several fixtures, the restriction may be in your lateral or in the city's main. Barberton's utilities department at 576 W Park Ave can tell you how the line is divided on your street.

The city's stormwater information describes combined, sanitary and storm sewers, and a main under strain after a storm can slow house drains. If the slowness comes only after heavy rain, tell the city and your plumber, because the cause may not be in your house.

Source: City of Barberton utilities and stormwater pages.

Air gaps and high loops

Many dishwashers drain through an air gap or a high loop in the hose that keeps dirty water from flowing back. If the air gap on the sink deck clogs, the dishwasher can back into the sink.

Pull the cap and clean the air gap with a small brush. If the hose has a sag or a kink, straighten it, and make sure the high loop is secured under the counter.

Double bowls and shared traps

Two sinks that share one trap drain together, so a partial blockage shows up as slow draining in both bowls. When you drain one bowl and the other gurgles, the shared line is restricted.

A plumber can clear the line from the cleanout and the trap. If the sinks are slow only when both are full, the pipe is likely undersized or partly blocked.

A belly in the pipe

A sagging section of drain pipe holds water and collects solids. It drains slowly and clogs repeatedly because the water cannot carry debris past the low point.

A camera inspection finds the belly. Depending on the location, the fix is to re-pitch the pipe or replace a section. Our camera inspection can check your line.

A quick home test

Fill the bowl with a few inches of water and let it drain. Time it and listen. A smooth, quiet drain is healthy, while a slow drain with gurgling points to a restriction or vent trouble.

Repeat with the dishwasher draining into the sink. If it slows only then, the issue is in the dishwasher connection or the disposal inlet.

Why chemical cleaners are a poor fix

Chemical cleaners eat through a clog in a narrow spot, but they leave grease on the walls and sit in the trap if the drain stays slow. They can damage some pipes and finishes and are a risk to anyone who opens the line afterward.

A mechanical cleaning removes the film and does not rely on harsh chemicals. If the sink is slow again soon after cleaning, schedule a jetting visit.

What a plumber checks first

A plumber starts at the sink, checks the trap and the disposal inlet, then moves to the cleanout or the nearest access point. They watch how fast water leaves and listen for gurgling.

Clear information helps. Tell them when the slowness started, whether it changes with rain and whether other fixtures are slow too.

Why venting matters

Under the Ohio Plumbing Code, built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), every trap needs a vent so water can flow without pulling air through the drain. A sink that drains slowly even after cleaning is worth a vent check.

Prevent the next slowdown

Wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing, collect used cooking fat in a can and rinse the drain with hot water after washing dishes. Avoid pouring hot oil down the sink.

A yearly drain cleaning visit helps older kitchens where grease has built over the years. We serve Barberton, Norton and Copley.
