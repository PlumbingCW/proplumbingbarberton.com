Sewer line emergency: signs you cannot wait

A failed sewer line can flood a basement without warning. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, a backup in a living space gets the next available plumber, and while you wait you should stop using water and keep the washer and dishwasher off.

Stay out of any wet area. Do not run a rented machine through the line, because a broken pipe can make the damage much worse.

Warning signs of a damaged sewer line

These are the patterns that send us to the camera.

  • ✓The same drain backs up every few months
  • ✓A depression or soggy patch in the yard along the pipe path
  • ✓Sewage odor outside near the cleanout
  • ✓Slow drains in several fixtures that cabling only fixes briefly
  • ✓Cracks in the foundation wall near the line entry

Why laterals fail in older Barberton neighborhoods

Barberton's original settlement sits in a valley parallel to the Tuscarawas River and the old Ohio and Erie Canal corridor. The city was founded in 1891, and laterals from the early and mid twentieth century were often clay or cast iron.

Clay joints let roots in. Cast iron scales and thins from the inside. Shifting soil in a wet valley can offset a joint, so even a sound pipe may sag over time. These are the three failures we see on older lots.

Permits and who to call

Barberton's building department has handled plumbing permits and registered plumbing contractors, and in 2026 the city announced that Summit County's Division of Building Standards takes over state-required building code enforcement. The announcement does not name plumbing permits, so call the city or county before you assume which office issues yours.

Ask any plumber to confirm their state license before you sign. Summit County's contractor tips say to ask whether a contractor is licensed by the State of Ohio.
