Where North Barberton sits

North Barberton lies less than a mile north of downtown Barberton, in ZIP 44203. Real-estate guides place its main roads at West State Street, Summit Road, Norton Avenue and Barber Road, with shops and restaurants along North Wooster Road and Fourth Street NW.

Barberton Middle and High School and Rudy Sharkey Stadium are here, and Newton Park and Decker Park are the neighborhood's larger green spaces. Those names help when you tell a plumber where you are, because street numbers alone can be hard to place over the phone.

What one local guide says about the housing

A real-estate neighborhood guide for North Barberton lists a median build year of 1933 and describes mostly small lots of under a quarter acre. It mentions early 1900s cottages, Cape Cods and bungalows laid out in a grid, with alleys behind many houses.

That is a description of the street pattern and not a finding about the pipes. We never assume what is inside your walls, so the first step on any visit is to look at your actual lines, fixtures and water heater.

Basements, storm water and the sump pump

The same guide notes that large parts of North Barberton are in a flood zone, and Barberton's June 2019 flooding is a reminder that storm water reaches basements here. You can check your own address on FEMA's flood map service, and your insurer can explain what it covers.

A working sump pump with a battery backup is the usual first line of defense for a basement that takes on water. If water keeps coming in after the pump is working, the cause may be groundwater and not plumbing, and our basement waterproofing page explains how to tell.

Working in tight lots and older layouts

Compact lots mean pipes, shutoff valves and cleanouts can sit close to neighboring properties and in tight basements. A plumber who can see the layout first can plan access, protect finished spaces and give you a clearer price.

Detached garages reached by an alley are common in the area, according to the guide. If you want water, a floor drain or a utility sink in a garage, ask us on the phone and we will explain what the job involves before work begins.

Before we arrive in North Barberton

A few minutes of preparation saves time on any visit.

1

Find the main shutoff

Most basements have a main water valve where the line enters the house. Closing it stops the flow to every fixture.

2

Clear the work area

Move boxes and stored items away from the water heater, the floor drain and the sump pit.

3

Note what you see

Write down which drains are slow, when the problem began and any smells or sounds.

4

Keep water use low

If a drain is backing up, hold off on laundry, showers and flushing until a plumber has looked.
