What to tell the dispatcher

Give your street address, the fixture or pipe involved and whether water is still flowing. Say if sewage is present and whether anyone in the home is at risk.

If you smell gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher confirms your address and the problem, tells you the price structure and books the next available plumber. For emergencies, same-day service is common.

You will get a call or text from the plumber when they are on the way. If the situation changes while you wait, call back and tell the dispatcher.
