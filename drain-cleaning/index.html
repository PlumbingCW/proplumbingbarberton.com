When a clogged drain is an emergency

If water is rising in a tub, a toilet will not flush, or a basement floor drain is gurgling, treat it as urgent. Our dispatch line runs 24/7 and a plumber can often be booked the same day, and while you wait you should stop running water in the house and keep the washer and dishwasher off.

Lift rugs away from the wet area and keep children out of the room. Do not pour chemical cleaner into a standing backup, because it sits in the pipe and can burn skin when the plumber opens the line.

Signs the clog is deeper than one fixture

A single slow sink is usually a local clog. When more than one drain misbehaves, the blockage has moved downstream and a hand plunger will not reach it.

  • ✓Two or more fixtures drain slowly at the same time
  • ✓A toilet bubbles when the shower or washer runs
  • ✓Water shows up in the lowest tub or floor drain
  • ✓A sour smell returns a day after you clear the sink
  • ✓Gurgling comes from a drain far from the one in use

What causes drain trouble in Barberton homes

Barberton was founded in 1891 and its population peaked at 33,805 in 1960, so many neighborhoods have homes with drain lines laid decades ago. Older cast iron and clay lines roughen on the inside, so grease and hair catch more easily than they do in smooth plastic.

Tree roots are the other repeat offender on older lots. If your clog keeps coming back every few months, the cause is often a root intrusion or a sagging section, and a camera look is cheaper than a fifth snaking visit. Our camera sewer inspection shows exactly what is in the pipe.

Kitchen sinks, tubs and floor drains

Kitchen lines clog with cooking fat that cools into a paste, so a hot-water flush only moves the problem down the pipe. A cable and, for stubborn grease, hydro jetting scours the wall clean.

Tub and shower drains hold hair wrapped around the stopper or trap arm. We pull the hair out rather than pushing it deeper, which is why a hand tool and a short cable often fix a shower faster than a big machine.

Basement floor drains deserve their own mention. When a floor drain backs up during heavy rain, the trouble can sit in the sewer rather than your plumbing, and a sewer line cleaning visit is the right next step.

What to do before we arrive

Stop running water into the clogged drain, because every flush or load of laundry pushes more waste toward the low point. Keep children and pets away from any floor drain that is bubbling or backing up.

Do not pour chemical drain openers into a drain that is already full of standing water. They can splash back on skin and they make a plumber's job riskier.

Standards for a clean drain job

Every fixture needs a trap and a vent under the Ohio Plumbing Code, which builds on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). A drain that gurgles often points to a venting problem and not only a clog.

What you can safely try first

A flange plunger on a toilet or a cup plunger on a sink works for a simple clog. Give it a dozen firm strokes, and stop if water stays high or nothing moves.

Skip drain snakes from the hardware aisle on a main line. A rented machine can tear an old pipe, and a torn clay joint can turn a simple clog into a sewer line repair. If a second plunger attempt fails, call.
